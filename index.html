About me

Kristen Schumacher Aloh

My work spans the gravitational-wave spectrum, across ground-based detectors (LVK), future space-based detectors (LISA), and pulsar timing arrays (NANOGrav). Passionate about making astrophysics accessible, I also develop visualization tools to help both experts and non-experts understand complex concepts and to inspire curiosity about the universe.

Currently I am exploring:

  • potential methods to place constraints on additional polarizations of gravitational waves
  • gravitational waves as cosmological probes
  • gravitational wave constraints on the equation of state of neutron stars

VR & visualization

Polarizations of gravitational waves

Though waves in general relativity contain only two polarizations, waves in modified theories of gravity may consist of up to six polarizations. Under my leadership the POINT collaboration developed animations which illustrate how the different polarizations of gravitational waves distort spacetime.

To use these animations in talks, please credit [Schumacher et al., ISVC 2024; POINT collaboration].
